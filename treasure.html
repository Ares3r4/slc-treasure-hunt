🎄 CMS Christmas treasure hunt 🎁

Hosted by the Student Learning Community

Objective

The purpose of this event is to organize a fun and engaging treasure hunt activity for members of CMS. It encourages problem-solving and exploration while bringing a festive and exciting challenge across campus.

Event Overview

The Treasure Hunt features five hidden locations around the campus. Each location can be found by scanning QR codes that lead to digital puzzles. Each puzzle is a, clue for the next location .

How It Works

  • Start at the designated starting point and scan the first QR code.
  • Each QR code opens a webpage with a puzzle of a familiar campus location.
  • Continue through all five stages to complete the hunt.
  • The first participant to finish all puzzles and reach the final spot wins!

Rules

  • This is a solo event — participants must complete all puzzles on their own.
  • Use your phone to scan QR codes and solve the puzzles.
  • Please do not move or damage QR codes placed on campus.
  • Fair play and honesty are expected throughout the game.
  • To claim the prize, the winner must show proof of all five puzzles solved.

Outcome

The event brings festive excitement and campus exploration to the SLC community. Expect an evening of fun, discovery, and holiday spirit as you race to find the treasure! 🎅✨
